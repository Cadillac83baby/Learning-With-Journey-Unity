## New requirement before ABC World
Each game should have a real progression system that advances through multiple levels. Target maximum is **5 or 10 levels per game**. This is not implemented yet and is the next design/code task before moving on to ABC World.

### Counting World progression should eventually include
- Level 1 through either Level 5 or Level 10.
- Increasing difficulty instead of repeating identical rounds indefinitely.
- Level completion criteria and a clear transition to the next level.
- Persistent saved progress so a child can return later.
- Rewards/points integrated with level completion.
- A finished-game state after the final level rather than looping forever.
